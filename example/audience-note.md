# Audience Note

**Fabricated instrument. Illustration only.**

## Who it genuinely helps

SME owners and founders about to open or switch a business account — newly licensed companies especially, and anyone whose application has been sitting in limbo without explanation. Slides 3, 4, 7 and 9 are written for them.

Also SME finance managers and CFOs. The closure fee, forced-bundle and notice-period points are direct line items they can act on this quarter.

## Who will actually engage

Bank relationship managers, compliance staff, and operations leads. Their job changes on the effective date, and the closing question is aimed squarely at them — it is binary, it is addressed to their role, and answering it signals competence to their own network.

Then the advisers: business setup consultants, corporate service providers, accountants and lawyers with SME clients. They will reshare rather than comment, because it is a conversation they are already having with clients.

## Who it is not for

- Large corporates — this instrument is SME-specific.
- Retail consumers — covered by a separate regime.
- Anyone outside the jurisdiction.

Stating the non-audience keeps the copy from hedging to cover people it was never written for. Hedged copy is what makes regulatory posts unreadable.

## Funnel position — the honest bit

This is a **top-of-funnel authority post, not lead generation.**

It will earn reach and credibility with a finance and banking audience. If what you sell is marketing and business development services to professional-services firms, that audience is adjacent to your buyer, not identical to them. Expect engagement from people who will not buy.

That is a legitimate thing to publish. It is not a failure. But know which one you are getting before you publish, not after you count the leads.

## Planned follow-up

Publish within two weeks, while the attention is still warm: a post on a topic sitting closer to the buyer, with the same sourcing rigour. The first post buys credibility with a wider audience; the second spends it.

Do not publish the first without having decided the second.
