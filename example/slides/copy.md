## 02 — Context

**Head:** What changes
**Body:** Example Central Bank issued Circular No. 2 of 2026 in February. It takes effect 13 September 2026 and replaces the 2021 circular.
**Ref:** Circular No. 2 of 2026

## 03 — Protection

**Head:** 3 business days
**Body:** To open an SME account, once your application file is complete.
**Ref:** Art. 4.2

## 04 — Protection

**Head:** 14 days maximum
**Body:** Any longer delay requires a written reason, given to you.
**Ref:** Art. 4.5

## 05 — Protection

**Head:** No closure fee
**Body:** After six months, leaving costs nothing.
**Ref:** Art. 6.1

## 06 — Protection

**Head:** No forced bundles
**Body:** A bank cannot require an unrelated product as a condition of opening.
**Ref:** Art. 6.4

## 07 — The turn

**Head:** Here's the part most founders miss
**Body:** The three-day clock does not start when you apply. It starts when your file is complete.
**Ref:** Art. 4.3

*This is the spine of the deck. Largest type after the cover.*

## 08 — What that means

**Head:** The protection is conditional
**Body:** Documents missing or wrong, and the deadline never begins to run. Where enhanced financial-crime checks apply, it is waived outright. The obligation sits with the bank — you have to qualify for it.
**Ref:** Art. 4.7

## 09 — Readiness checklist

**Head:** Before 13 September
**Body:** Trade licence · Constitutional documents · ID and passport for every shareholder · Proof of address · One page on what the business does and where the money comes from
**Callout:** That last document is where most applications fail.

## 10 — Considered close

**Body:** I have watched founders lose months waiting on an account with no explanation. That there is now a legal deadline on it, no exit fee, and a named body to escalate to tells you something about how seriously this market takes the people building in it.
