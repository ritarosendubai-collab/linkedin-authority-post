## Decision 1 — Tone register for the closing line

Presented to the human. Not auto-selected.

**Option A — Warmer**

> This is regulation doing what it should: spotting friction that slows entrepreneurs down and legislating it away. Few markets give small businesses a legal right to a three-day account opening, a free exit, and a named body to escalate to.

**Option B — Warmer still**

> Once again the standard is being set here. While most markets leave small businesses to negotiate with their banks alone, this regulator has written their rights into law — three days to open, freedom to leave, transparency on every fee.

**Option C — Personal** *(recommended)*

> I have watched founders lose months waiting on an account with no explanation. That there is now a legal deadline on it, no exit fee, and a named body to escalate to tells you something about how seriously this market takes the people building in it.

**Recommendation:** C. It carries the same warmth without superlatives, and because it is grounded in something only the writer has seen it reads as credible rather than promotional. It also gives the network something to reply to — people respond to experience far more readily than to assessment.

**Chosen:** C.
**Placement:** after the readiness checklist, before the closing question — so it lands as a considered close and warms the reader immediately before a pointed question.

## Decision 2 — Link placement

**Options:** source URL in-post near the bottom · URL in first comment.
**Chosen:** in-post, four lines from the bottom.
**Reason:** for a regulatory post the visible citation is worth more than the marginal reach. Flagged to the user that moving it to the first comment is the higher-reach option.

## Decision 3 — Post format

**Options:** document post (PDF) · image post (11 PNGs).
**Chosen:** document post, PNGs retained as fallback.
**Reason:** keeps the swipe interaction and typically reaches further. User to confirm the PDF preview renders correctly before publishing.

## Open — human owned

- **Image rights.** No asset selected in this example. In a real run, any licensed or third-party image must be confirmed cleared for commercial social use before publication. Currency imagery, official emblems and editorial-only stock all trip this gate.
- **Publish timing.** User picks a slot where they can spend the following hour replying.
