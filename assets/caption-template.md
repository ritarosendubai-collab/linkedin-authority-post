## Notes

- **First line is everything.** It is the only line guaranteed to be read before the "see more" truncation. Lead with the stake.
- **`{{one_honest_observation}}`** — something true and slightly uncomfortable: that almost nobody has prepared, that it replaced something weaker, that it is unusually strict. Not "this is an important development."
- **`{{optional_considered_line}}`** — the tone-calibrated close. Present three temperatures to the user; do not pick silently. See `references/tone-calibration.md`.
- **Source line placement.** Three or four lines from the bottom limits the external-link reach penalty. If reach matters more than the visible citation, move the URL to your own first comment — the slides still carry the article references.
- **`{{binary_question}}`** — aimed at the group whose job changes, answerable in one word. Not "what do you think?"
- **Three hashtags.** More does not help and looks like reach-chasing.
